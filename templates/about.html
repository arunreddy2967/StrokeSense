About StrokeSense

StrokeSense is an ML-based stroke risk assessment system designed to estimate stroke susceptibility using selected demographic, medical, and lifestyle information.

What is StrokeSense?

StrokeSense is a machine learning based application developed to provide a preliminary estimation of stroke risk from selected health and lifestyle characteristics.

The system processes information such as age, gender, hypertension, heart disease, BMI, average glucose level, smoking status, marital status, work type, and residence type. The information is processed by a trained classification model to produce an estimated risk probability and risk category.

StrokeSense is designed as a supportive risk-assessment tool and is not intended to replace professional medical evaluation or diagnosis.

What is Brain Stroke?

A stroke occurs when the blood supply to part of the brain is interrupted or reduced. When brain cells do not receive enough oxygen and nutrients, they can become damaged.

Stroke is a serious medical condition that can result in neurological problems affecting movement, speech, and other functions. Recognizing warning signs quickly and obtaining appropriate medical attention is important.

Ischemic Stroke

An ischemic stroke occurs when a blood vessel supplying the brain becomes blocked, reducing or stopping blood flow to part of the brain.

Hemorrhagic Stroke

A hemorrhagic stroke occurs when a blood vessel in the brain ruptures and causes bleeding within or around brain tissue.

Stroke Risk Assessment Attributes

StrokeSense uses the following attributes from the Stroke Prediction Dataset to generate a preliminary risk estimate.

Attribute Meaning Values / Condition
Age Age of the individual in years. Numerical value.
Gender Gender recorded for the individual. Male, Female, Other.
Hypertension Indicates whether the individual has hypertension. Yes / No.
Heart Disease Indicates whether the individual has a recorded heart disease condition. Yes / No.
Average Glucose Level Average blood glucose measurement associated with the individual. Numerical value.
BMI Body Mass Index representing a measure based on body weight and height. Numerical value.
Smoking Status Smoking status recorded for the individual. Never smoked, Formerly smoked, Smokes, Unknown.
Ever Married Marital status recorded in the dataset. Yes / No.
Work Type Employment category of the individual. Private, Self-employed, Govt job, Children, Never worked.
Residence Type Type of residence recorded for the individual. Urban / Rural.

Understanding the Assessment Conditions

The assessment form allows the user to provide values for different health and lifestyle attributes. These values are converted into a format that can be processed by the trained machine learning pipeline.

Hypertension

Select Yes when hypertension is present and No when it is not recorded.

Heart Disease

Select Yes when heart disease is present and No otherwise.

Smoking

Select the available category that represents the individual's recorded smoking status.

BMI

Enter the BMI value available for the individual. Missing BMI values in the training data are handled during preprocessing.

Glucose Level

Enter the average glucose level associated with the individual's health record.

Age

Enter the individual's age in years. Age is processed as a numerical feature by the ML pipeline.

Recognizing Stroke Warning Signs — FAST

FAST is a simple way of remembering important warning signs associated with stroke.

F

Face

Look for sudden facial weakness or drooping.

A

Arm

Look for sudden weakness or difficulty raising an arm.

S

Speech

Look for sudden speech difficulty or unclear speech.

T

Time

Seek emergency medical attention immediately when stroke symptoms occur.

How StrokeSense Works

The system follows a sequence of steps to transform the entered health information into a preliminary risk estimate.

1

Health Information

User enters assessment details.

2

Data Processing

Input is prepared for the ML pipeline.

3

ML Prediction

The trained model estimates risk probability.

4

Risk Category

The probability is evaluated using the selected decision threshold.

5

Assessment Result

The estimated risk is displayed and stored.

Important Health Information

StrokeSense provides an estimated ML-based risk assessment based on the information entered by the user. The result is intended for preliminary assessment and educational or decision-support purposes. It does not diagnose stroke and does not replace evaluation by a qualified healthcare professional. If someone experiences sudden symptoms associated with stroke, seek emergency medical attention immediately.
